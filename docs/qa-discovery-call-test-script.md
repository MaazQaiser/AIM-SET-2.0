# QA Discovery Call Script (~2 minutes)

Tuned so live BANT, pains, sentiment, Co-Pilot, email, and Jira land cleanly.
Speak at a normal pace (~90–120 seconds).

**Pre-DC:** open the brief — confirm today’s call + company load.  
**Live:** say the lines below in order (Buyer lines matter most).  
**Post-DC:** check BANT, short email, Jira.

---

## Live dialogue

**AE:** Thanks for joining — what’s driving this evaluation?

**Buyer:** I’m a bit concerned we’ve been burned by vendors before.

**Buyer:** Biggest pain point: spreadsheet plus QuickBooks is causing payroll errors and invoicing disputes.

**Buyer:** We need an operations platform for scheduling, payroll, billing, and incident reporting. This is a top priority and a must have.

**Buyer:** I have the final say and full decision authority on this purchase.

**Buyer:** The approved budget is 10-50K for year one.

**Buyer:** Timeline is the main constraint. Kickoff within 2 weeks, decision by Friday, and go-live by end of the month.

**AE:** Confirming: budget 10-50K, you have the final say, kickoff within two weeks, decision by Friday, go-live by end of the month.

**Buyer:** Yes — those dates work.

**Buyer:** Do you have a relevant case study or project for operations platforms like this?

**AE:** *(ask Co-Pilot)* Show the most relevant case studies and projects for this call.

**Buyer:** Next step: send a discovery proposal and a security one-pager, then schedule a technical review.

**AE:** I’ll send the proposal and one-pager, and schedule the review.

**Buyer:** Perfect — I’m excited to move forward.

---

## Expected outputs

| Area | Should show |
|------|-------------|
| **Sentiment** | Concern early → excited / positive at the end |
| **Pain** | Spreadsheet + QuickBooks payroll / invoicing disputes |
| **Need** | Operations platform (scheduling, payroll, billing, incidents) |
| **Authority** | Final say / full decision authority |
| **Budget** | **10-50K** (full range) |
| **Timeline** | Within 2 weeks, decision by Friday, go-live by end of the month |
| **Co-Pilot** | Case study / project answer (if KB has projects) |
| **Email** | Short intro + Key takeaways + Action items (proposal, one-pager, schedule review) |
| **Jira** | Short bullets; BANT chips match Post-DC (all four confirmed) |
